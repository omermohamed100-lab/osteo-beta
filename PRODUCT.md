# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Patients, students, osteopathic practitioners, clinicians, educators, and potential partners in Egypt and the Middle East. Visitors need to understand osteopathy, trust EGSOM as a professional society, discover education and events, and find qualified practitioners.

## Product Purpose

Present the Egyptian Society of Osteopathic Medicine as a credible regional authority, explain its mission clearly, and connect visitors with education, practitioners, activities, and contact channels. Success means the site feels immediately trustworthy, humane, and established.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, protective, and authoritative. The brand should feel medically credible without becoming cold, and premium without becoming ornamental.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic hospital-template imagery, exaggerated futuristic medical interfaces, visual clutter, or a wholesale redesign that loses the established EGSOM identity. Do not make the site feel like a technology startup or an impersonal medical portal.

## Design Principles

- Lead with human care and professional confidence.
- Make osteopathy understandable through clear, meaningful imagery.
- Preserve the society's established navy, white, and silver identity.
- Keep each visual decision focused on trust, clarity, and regional credibility.
- Respect existing content and structure unless a change is explicitly requested.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, responsive layouts, keyboard-accessible controls, clear image alternatives, and reduced-motion support. The site must support both English and Arabic, including right-to-left reading, in the planned localization phase.
